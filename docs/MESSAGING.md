# Shopfront messaging

Public wording for a landing page, a talk, or a post. Lead with the café job. The Hostinger note at the bottom is a distribution detail, not the pitch.

**Demo:** https://sienna-ant-771673.hostingersite.com/

## One sentence

Shopfront is the menu behind the QR sticker for a café that already has one and is tired of reprinting a PDF every time a price, an allergen, a sold-out item, or today’s special changes.

## Three bullets

- Guests scan once: English plus a second language, and allergen or diet filters, on their phone. No app.
- Staff mark sold out, set today’s special, change a price, or add a photo from their phone. The sticker stays.
- WhatsApp, Maps, and Instagram sit on the menu. One Docker Compose stack or a Node process, one SQLite file. Display only — no cart, no payment, no kitchen tickets.

## Why not just a PDF?

A PDF is frozen when you export it. The next price change is a new file and, if the QR pointed at that file, a new sticker. Shopfront keeps one URL. The sticker stays on the table. The menu moves.

## Why not a POS?

A POS takes the order and the money. Shopfront does not, on purpose. If the shop needs a cart, tickets, or payments, use a POS. If the shop needs the table QR to show today’s real menu, use Shopfront.

## Words to keep

display-only, today’s menu, the sticker stays, from your phone, bilingual, allergens, sold out, not a checkout.

## Words that send the wrong shop

cart, checkout, order at the table, kitchen display, “free POS”, “replace your register”.

## 一句话

Shopfront 是已经在桌上贴了二维码的咖啡馆用的菜单：客人扫一次，店员用手机改价格、过敏原、售罄和今日特选，贴纸不用为了改内容而重印。

## 三条

- 客人手机上看菜单：英文加第二种语言，一键切换；可按过敏原和饮食筛选。不用装 App。
- 店员在手机上标售罄、设今日特选、改价格、传菜品图。二维码贴纸不动。
- 菜单上有 WhatsApp、地图和 Instagram。一台机器、一个 SQLite 文件，Docker Compose 或 Node 都能跑。只展示，不点单、不收款、不出后厨票。

## 为什么不用 PDF？

PDF 一导出就停在那一天。下次改价就要新文件，二维码如果指向那个文件，贴纸也得换。Shopfront 的网址不变，贴纸留在桌上，菜单在变。

## 为什么不用 POS？

POS 负责点单和收钱。Shopfront 故意不做这些。店需要购物车、小票或付款，就用 POS。店需要桌上的二维码一直显示今天的真菜单，就用 Shopfront。
